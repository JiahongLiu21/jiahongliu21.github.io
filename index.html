    About Me

    I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), supervised by Prof. Irwin King.

    Previously, I received an M.S. in Computer Science and Technology from Harbin Institute of Technology under the supervision of Prof. Philippe Fournier-Viger, and visited the National University of Singapore in 2025, working closely with Prof. Tat-Seng Chua. I also have a close collaboration with Prof. Rex Ying at Yale University.

    My research focuses on personalized and structure-aware foundation models, with an emphasis on non-Euclidean geometry, user preferences, and long-term memory.

    Research Interests

    • Non-Euclidean geometry: hyperbolic learning, structural priors, and multimodal continual learning.
    • Personalized foundation models: preference modeling, representation-space personalization, and efficient adaptation.
    • Agents, memory, and retrieval: long-term personalization and user-specific knowledge.

    I welcome conversations and collaborations on these topics. Feel free to contact me!
